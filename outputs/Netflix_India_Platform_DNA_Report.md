## POSITIONING

Netflix India is the deliberate choice in a market of defaults — the service urban viewers open when they want something worth their time rather than something merely available. Its role in the Indian stack is curator and quality signal: the thali host whose picks travel to 190-plus countries while rivals fight over bundles and cricket calendars. Its conviction is total on craft and curation: 200 Originals filmed in 100 locations across India, an Emmy-winning police procedural in Delhi Crime, and a 2026 slate stretching from the Tamil series Legacy, debuted 14 January 2026, to Heeramandi season 2. Its narrowness is reach and price: roughly 20 million paying subscribers against a 601.2 million OTT universe, a ₹149 to ₹649 ladder with no annual pack, and APAC revenue per membership of $7.31 against $17.20 in the US and Canada. It is a deliberate alternative to the telecom-bundled mass utility and the e-commerce-bundled Prime Video — smaller in India, but tied at 22% with Prime Video on JustWatch Q2 2026 engagement while monetising at a premium.

## PITCH PLAYBOOK

**PITCH ANGLE:** Lead with craft and global travel, not reach. Netflix India buys premium-grade storytelling with festival or breakout potential and a clear path to trending rows in 190-plus countries — the thali needs one prestige anchor and one velocity play every quarter, and the 2026 Tamil and Telugu expansion is the most open lane.

**IDEAL TITLE PROFILE:** A Hindi, Tamil or Telugu crime, thriller, romance or prestige-drama series or film with a distinctive creator voice, cinematic production values, and dubbed or subtitled travel potential, structured for a limited series or a renewable multi-season arc.

**INSTANT YES SIGNALS:**
- ✅ Prestige crime or thriller series in the Sacred Games or Delhi Crime mould
- ✅ Star-led Tamil or Telugu originals fitting the six-title 2025 expansion slate
- ✅ Female-led dramas with breakout potential like Heeramandi or Dabba Cartel
- ✅ True-story adaptations with cinematic scale such as Black Warrant or IC 814
- ✅ Comedy-variety formats with renewal potential like The Great Indian Kapil Show
- ✅ Young-adult romance or campus stories in the Mismatched mould
- ✅ Post-theatrical Hindi or regional blockbusters with day-and-date dubbed versions
- ✅ Documentary or docu-drama with global festival pedigree like The Elephant Whisperers

**INSTANT NO SIGNALS:**
- ❌ Subtitle-only single-language pitches with no dubbing or travel plan beyond one state
- ❌ Purely volume-driven daily-soap formats built for simulcast rather than binge rows
- ❌ One-off reality formats dependent on live voting or commerce integrations Netflix does not operate
- ❌ Sports rights or live-event properties — Netflix holds no major Indian sports rights
- ❌ High-cost star vehicles with no distinctive creator voice or script locked before shoot
- ❌ Niche festival-only arthouse with no path to the trending rows in India
- ❌ Content requiring windowed or non-exclusive carve-outs across rival Indian platforms
- ❌ Projects needing telecom-bundle distribution to find an audience

**NEGOTIATION INTELLIGENCE:** Netflix runs a co-CEO structure, with Ted Sarandos in place since July 2020 and India content led by Monika Shergill since July 2019, so scripted approvals route through Mumbai with global content oversight rather than a broadcast-style entertainment CEO. As a single global buyer it typically seeks broad exclusive streaming rights for its originals while licensing is title-by-title, as the 2026 Universal Studios and Sony Pictures expansions show. Title-level performance data is shared selectively through top-10 and views disclosures such as the 120 million views reported for Stranger Things, so sellers should negotiate measurement and exclusivity windows as explicit deal terms rather than assume granular data will be volunteered.
