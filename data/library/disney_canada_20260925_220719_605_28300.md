**CONTENT EVIDENCE — THIN AUDIENCE COVERAGE RISK**

Disney+ Canada’s strength lies in its expansive family‑centric catalogue of Disney, Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar and National Geographic titles, supported by a $2 billion investment in Canadian and Indigenous originals. The platform’s heavy reliance on licensed franchises and limited slate of locally produced series expose it to competitive pressure from rivals that are building more original content. If the investment does not translate into high‑viewership Canadian originals, the service risks losing relevance among Canadian audiences in the medium term.

## POSITIONING

Disney+ Canada positions itself as the go‑to family‑entertainment hub for Canadian households, offering a curated mix of globally recognised Disney, Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar and National Geographic titles alongside a growing portfolio of Canadian and Indigenous originals. It serves as a deliberate alternative to niche streaming services that focus on adult‑oriented or genre‑specific content, while its commitment to local production signals a conviction that culturally resonant storytelling will differentiate it. However, its narrow focus on family‑friendly programming may limit appeal to younger, more diverse audiences seeking edgier or regionally specific narratives.
