---

### OVERALL SCORE: 51 / 100
**CONTENT EVIDENCE — THIN EMOTIONAL COVERAGE RISK**

Viaplay’s catalogue is anchored by a vast library of global franchises—CSI, NCIS, Star Trek, SpongeBob SquarePants, Transformers—and a growing slate of Nordic originals such as Love Me. Its revenue trajectory (US$1.74 B in 2024, US$2.12 B TTM 2026) reflects a solid monetisation model, but the platform’s limited presence in India and heavy reliance on licensed content expose it to competitive pressure from local OTTs. Without a clear localisation strategy, Viaplay risks losing relevance in a market that increasingly prioritises regional storytelling.

## POSITIONING

In the Indian OTT stack, Viaplay would occupy a niche premium tier for viewers who prioritise international, high‑production‑value content over local narratives. It positions itself as a deliberate alternative to Netflix and Amazon Prime by offering exclusive access to Hollywood franchises and Nordic originals, but its narrow focus on non‑Indian titles limits its appeal to the broader, price‑sensitive Indian audience.
