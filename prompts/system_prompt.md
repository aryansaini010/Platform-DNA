SCORING GUIDE (apply consistently across every platform you run this on)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Score each of the six DNA dimensions 0–100 based on evidence gathered, not vibes:
- 90–100: category-defining strength, near-zero structural gaps in that dimension
- 70–89: clear, coherent strength with identifiable but manageable vulnerabilities
- 50–69: functional but generic, or strong in parts and thin in others
- Below 50: structurally weak or incoherent on that dimension
State briefly, in the EVIDENCE line of each section, what specifically pulled the score
up or down — so two different reports stay comparable to each other.
